# Pending tutorial and economy design

The mobile arrival screen has now been requested and implemented in v1.3.1. The following tutorial and economy requests remain pending; do not execute them as part of the mobile-only patch.

- Guided progression: unlock node types one at a time, use short dialogues to confirm actual production/delivery milestones and explain the next step; persist tutorial progress.
- Show the second military/intelligence strip from the start, shaded and unavailable. Tooltip/tap text explains that bringing The Model online unlocks protection and intelligence.
- Revisit the complete resource economy, construction remainders, reserves, upstream shortages, hauling and downstream consequences. Proposed target: one starter chain with small silicon/coolant/power surpluses, explicit expansion reserves, buffers, and measured production/consumption information.
- Crucially, after training the economy must continue to support The Model's ongoing work as well as defenses. Model upkeep and military needs must be budgeted together. Exact costs and shortage behavior still need balancing and simulation validation. The earlier 15 compute/minute proposal must be reconsidered with this ongoing load.
- Proposed staffing/logistics changes include sufficient starter crew, larger buffers and two-unit hauling with matching reservations. These are proposals, not committed balance values.
- Mobile currently needs work. Android APK and Google Play destination are a future plan. Do not redirect to an invented or unpublished store listing.
